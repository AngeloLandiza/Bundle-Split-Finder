# Step 18: what it costs, what it will cost, and a limit that holds

**Owner (Nov 25):** "can you do estimated, current, and datascience/ML backed predictions on the cost? i want this data
in the app along with a cost chart. this should include only software services like llm api, keepa monthly, etc.
everything please i need to make sure as a user i am spending within my budget. also have a max budget slider for
things. make it easy. and ofc if budget is approaching for that service send telegram msg and warn the user and if
something is expected to hit the budget or go beyond it then it wont run and we have a cost blocker."

## What counts
Software services only: SerpApi (a plan: a fixed price for a number of searches), Keepa (a subscription; tokens refill),
the AI provider (metered dollars), and for completeness the free ones with a limit (eBay calls, Telegram). Not goods,
not Amazon fees.

## Decisions
- **A price is entered by the owner, never assumed.** The app knows what was used (its ledgers) and what the AI calls
  cost (token prices); it does not know what he pays SerpApi or Keepa. Until he enters it the service is "price not
  entered" and left out of the dollar totals, which say so. A free SerpApi plan is known from the account check.
- **A limit per service, in the service's own unit, on a slider**: searches a month, Keepa tokens a month, AI dollars a
  month (the existing `ai.monthlyCapUsd`, which this replaces as the one place). And one total in dollars.
  The search reserve keeps its own meaning (CLAUDE.md rule 1); a limit can only make the app spend less.
- **The forecast is statistics on his own use, stated as a range.** Month-end use per service = used so far + the runs
  his schedule still has × what a run costs + the between-scan drip. With enough history (decided from the data) the
  per-run and per-day amounts are resampled with a seeded bootstrap to give a middle and a 10th to 90th percentile
  range; with too little, the forecast is the bound his settings allow and says so. No model that the data cannot
  support; the method is one sentence under the chart.
- **Warn, then hold.** At the warn percent (80 by default): one in-app notice, one system notification, one Telegram
  message, once a month per service. When the forecast passes the limit before the month ends: the same, with the day.
  **The cost blocker:** before a step that costs (a scan's lookups, a drip lookup, the listing check, review cards, a
  Verify), the app asks whether used + what the step is expected to use at the high end stays inside the limit; if
  not, the step does not run, the rest of the scan still does, and Activity, the notice and Telegram say what was held
  and what would let it run. The blocker never raises a limit by itself.
- **One screen, Costs**: this month in dollars against the total budget (a line so far, the forecast as a dashed line
  with its range, the budget as a rule), each service with its use against its limit, the sliders right there, and
  the numbers as a table for anyone who cannot read a chart.
